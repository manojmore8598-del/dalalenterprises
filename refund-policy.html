This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains how cancellations and refunds are handled for services provided by Dalal Enterprises Murbad. We aim to be fair and transparent with customers.

1 Introduction

Please read this policy before making payments for any service.

2 Service Booking and Payment

Payments may include (a) government fees payable to government departments/portals, and (b) service charges for local assistance, printing, Xerox, documentation support, or related work. Please confirm the nature of each payment before paying.

3 Cancellation Requests

Customers may request cancellation by contacting us via phone, WhatsApp, or by visiting the office. Whether a cancellation is possible depends on whether work has already started and whether any government fee has already been paid/submitted.

4 Refund Eligibility

Refund consideration generally applies to unprocessed local service charges where the requested assistance has not yet been started or completed. Eligibility is assessed case-by-case based on payment proof and service status.

5 Non-Refundable Services

  • Completed Xerox, printing, scanning, lamination, or similar consumable services.
  • Work already completed as requested by the customer.
  • Charges for time already spent on form filling/documentation once substantial work is done, unless otherwise agreed.

6 Government Fees

Government fees already paid to government departments or portals are generally non-refundable through us. Any refund of government fees must be handled according to the concerned department/portal rules, if available.

7 Application Rejection

If a government application is rejected by the concerned authority due to eligibility, document issues, departmental rules, or verification outcomes, government fees are typically not refundable. Local service charges already used for assistance/processing support may also be non-refundable once the service has been delivered.

8 Duplicate Payments

If a duplicate payment is made in error, contact us promptly with payment proof. We will review and help resolve eligible duplicate local service-charge payments. Duplicate government-portal payments must follow the portal’s own correction process.

9 Refund Processing Timeline

We do not publish a fixed refund timeline because processing depends on payment method, verification of records, and the specific case. After a refund is approved for an eligible local service charge, we will communicate the expected mode and approximate timing.

10 Refund Request Procedure

  1. Contact us with your name, mobile number, payment proof, and service details.
  2. Share the reason for cancellation/refund.
  3. Our team will verify service status and payment type (government fee vs service charge).
  4. We will inform you of the outcome and next steps.
