Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. Introduction

Dalal Enterprises Murbad (“we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use this website or request services at our offices.

2. Information We Collect

This website is a static informational website. It does not operate a backend database for storing customer form submissions. When you contact us via phone, WhatsApp, email, or visit our office, you may voluntarily share information needed for service assistance.

3. Personal Information

Depending on the enquiry or service, personal details may include name, mobile number, email address, address, and other details you choose to share.

4. Document Information

For government-related services, customers may provide copies of identity, address, income, or other supporting documents at the office. Document requirements depend on the concerned department and service.

Note: This website itself does not store uploaded customer documents, as no document-upload backend is implemented.

5. How We Use Information

  • To respond to service enquiries.
  • To assist with applications and documentation as requested by the customer.
  • To communicate regarding service status or required documents.
  • To improve customer support and office operations.

6. Information Sharing

We do not sell customer personal information. Information may be shared only as needed to process a requested service (for example, submitting details on a government portal as instructed by the customer) or when required by applicable law.

7. Government Portal and Third-Party Services

Many services involve third-party government portals (such as Maha e-Seva / Aaple Sarkar related systems) and communication platforms (WhatsApp, phone, email, Instagram). Those platforms and portals have their own privacy policies and terms, which are outside our control.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable care to protect information shared with us during service delivery. However, no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed as completely secure, and we do not claim absolute security certifications.

9. Data Retention

Information retained for service processing is kept only as long as reasonably needed for the requested work, follow-up, or legal/compliance needs. Retention may vary by service type and document necessity.

10. Customer Rights

You may request clarification about the information you have shared with us for a service, request updates to contact details, or ask us to stop further communication for marketing-style messages. For government applications already submitted to departments/portals, changes must follow that department’s process.

11. Cookies and Website Analytics

This static website may rely on standard browser/hosting behavior. If analytics or cookies are added later by the hosting platform, this policy should be updated accordingly. Currently, no advanced tracking system is claimed.

12. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
